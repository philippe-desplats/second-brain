# Catalog Search

## Observations

- [definition] The discovery flow that lets a buyer find one product inside a large catalog, combining a search box, filters, and the result layout
- [example] The Globex storefront, where trade buyers arrive with a part number and abandon when the first screen cannot confirm the match
- [opinion] On a professional catalog, exact-reference lookup comes first and faceted browsing is the fallback, not the reverse
- [reference] Progressive disclosure in B2B search interfaces, Example Journal, 2026-05-30
