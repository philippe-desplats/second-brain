# Lead Sourcing

## Observations

- [definition] The arrangement by which a third party sends qualified prospects, in exchange for a fee, a share of the engagement, or reciprocity
- [example] The Proseware partnership, where the referral fee is fixed per signed engagement rather than as a share of revenue
- [opinion] A referral agreement is worth writing down before the first lead, not after: the awkward conversation is about the second one, when the terms are already implied by how the first was handled
- [question] Does a lead that arrives twice, once through a partner and once directly, count as sourced? Answer it in the agreement rather than in the invoice
