## Partnership nature

Mixed partnership, commercial and technical, formalized as an affiliation contract on 2026-06-05 after two months of informal referrals. Proseware installs and maintains ERP and order-management systems for industrial SMEs; we build the customer-facing side. Neither side sells what the other sells, which is exactly what makes the referral flow safe.

Two active tracks. **Lead referral** runs both ways: Proseware sends web and e-commerce work it cannot take, we send back the ERP and order-flow work we do not touch. **Technical coordination** applies to the accounts where both systems meet, so the order API has one owner instead of two opinions. The money flows one way only: 10% of the first engagement on a lead Proseware sends, nothing on the leads we send back. That asymmetry is deliberate, both sides preferred it to negotiating a symmetric rate on unequal volumes.
