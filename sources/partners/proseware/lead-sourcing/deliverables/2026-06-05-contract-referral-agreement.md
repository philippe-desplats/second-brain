# Mutual referral agreement, Proseware

One page, signed on 2026-06-05, replacing the informal arrangement both sides had been running since April 2026. It sets how a lead is introduced, what a referral costs, and what happens on an account where both companies work.

## Observations

- [commitment] Every referral is introduced by email, with a one-paragraph scope summary, before anyone quotes a number
- [commitment] The receiving side reports back whether the lead converted, within two weeks of the decision
- [clause] 10% of the first engagement, excl. tax, on leads Proseware sends; leads we send back carry no fee
- [clause] The fee covers the first engagement only, never renewals, extensions, or maintenance that follow it
- [clause] 12-month term, tacit renewal, 30 days written notice on either side
- [clause] Each side contracts directly with a shared account, no subcontracting and no exclusivity
- [item] Referral fee, 10% of the first engagement, invoiced once the client has paid us
- [risk] The agreement relies on disclosure rather than exclusivity, nothing stops either side from being approached directly
- [risk] The fee is asymmetric, one way only, so a shift in referral volume is the thing to watch at renewal
